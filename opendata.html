Open Data

Open Data Resources for Environmental Sound Analysis and Machine Learning
Content

This page collects the open datasets I created and maintain from my time at Tampere University.

Introduction

These datasets support research and development in DCASE-related domains — machine learning, acoustic scene analysis, environmental audio signal processing — and are freely available to keep that research transparent and reproducible.

Researchers, students, and developers are welcome to use them for non-commercial projects; just check the license that comes with each one. Every dataset ships with documentation to get you started quickly.

For a broader overview of publicly available datasets in the DCASE community, visit the DCASE Datalist (also maintained by me), a curated index featuring metadata, licensing information, and direct access links.
