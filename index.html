I am currently the Machine Learning Lead at Resoniks, alongside a Visiting Researcher position at Tampere University. I previously spent years there as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow, affiliated with the Tampere Institute for Advanced Study (Tampere IAS). My academic work focused on automatic content analysis of environmental audio — specifically acoustic scene understanding and sound event detection. More
